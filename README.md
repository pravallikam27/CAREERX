# CAREERX – AI Career Intelligence

CAREERX is a career intelligence platform designed to help students and entry-level job seekers understand their readiness for technology roles.

## 🚀 Features

- 🔐 User Registration & Login
- 🎯 Job Match & Skill Gap Analysis
- 🎤 Mock Interview Practice
- 🗺️ Personalized Skill Roadmap
- 📚 Career Resources
- 📊 Explainable Job Match Score

## 💡 Problem

Students often find it difficult to understand how well their skills match a specific job description and what skills they need to improve.

CAREERX provides a simple platform to compare candidate skills with job requirements and identify areas for improvement.
